SITE INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy describes how information may be handled when you visit the personal website of Mohamed Gamal Ashry. The site is a static portfolio and does not currently provide user accounts, a contact form, or built-in analytics.

Information handled when you visit

The site itself does not ask you to submit personal information. Like most websites, the hosting provider may process technical request information, such as your IP address, browser, and the page requested, to deliver and protect the site. If the site is hosted using GitHub Pages, see GitHub’s Privacy Statement for information about its practices.

Fonts and external services

This website loads Space Grotesk and DM Mono fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive technical information associated with the request. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

The website also links to LinkedIn. Following an external link takes you to that service, which handles information according to its own policies.

Contact by email

If you choose the email link, your email application opens a message addressed to mga.ashry@gmail.com. Any information you include is sent through your email provider and shared with me so I can respond.

Cookies and analytics

The website source currently includes no analytics scripts or advertising trackers. The hosting provider or linked third-party services may use their own technical storage or logging; consult their privacy information for details.

Changes and questions

Site features and this policy may change. This page will be updated if the website begins collecting information directly, for example through a contact form or analytics tool. For privacy questions, email Mohamed Gamal Ashry.
